VYREN is an early-stage automotive project centered on the Aurora R26. Today, the project is focused on design, presentation and planning the path from a digital model to a physical prototype.

The site intentionally avoids presenting unfinished engineering, specifications or partnerships as established facts. As the project develops, this story will develop with it.

Now

Brand, concept and digital vehicle development.

Next

Physical scale model and prototype planning.

Then

Low-speed demonstrator and real-world development.

Future

Defined by what the project proves along the way.
